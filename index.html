Yard Fire

You spawn with just a knife. Guns and swords lie scattered through the jungle — walk up to one and press E to grab it. Rarer finds hit harder. Hold W against a watchtower ladder to climb it. Other players around means it's a deathmatch.

WASD move · Space jump (hold Space on a ladder to climb) · Shift sprint · Mouse aim · Left-click attack (sword: dash) · Right-click scope, or swing the sword · R reload · E pick up / swap weapon
Touch: left thumb moves, right thumb aims.
